Expertise

At STB, two questions guide Fabiana’s work: what does the student want to learn, and what needs to be in place for them to study abroad?

Education programs

Language and career programs

As STB’s Language & Career manager, Fabiana assesses how a course fits a student’s goals. For someone with only a few weeks abroad, she considers both classroom time and opportunities to use the language elsewhere. In Cape Town, for example, she explains how speaking with local winemakers becomes part of learning English.

Operations

From choosing a course to departure

Fabiana guides students and families through choices about courses, accommodation and flights. Presenting STB’s options to lawyers with children, she explained how parents and children can attend age-appropriate classes and what help the agency provides with documents and travel preparations.

Partnerships

Working with international schools

Fabiana brings together the perspectives of overseas schools and the people advising students in Brazil. With EC English and ILSC, she discussed combining study and work abroad. With Good Hope Studies and STB’s quality team, she explored what people over 50 look for in a study abroad program.

A partnership in practice

CAASP and STB

In September 2024, Fabiana joined STB’s commercial and education relations teams at a meeting with CAASP, the São Paulo lawyers’ assistance fund. She presented options for lawyers at different career stages, from legal English to executive education, and addressed the needs of those traveling with family.
